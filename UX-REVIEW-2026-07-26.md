# UX / bug review — Migracijski atlas · found and fixed

Date: 2026-07-26 · reviewed at `4c7c723` (v2.0.2) · fixed in v2.0.3
Method: full source read + four scripted puppeteer probes against the production
build, 390–1600 px. Every claim is a measurement; bracketed values are probe output.

**Suite: 107 → 134 checks, all green.** Each fix below is pinned by a named check,
so the "verifying check" column is the regression guard, not a description.

---

## P1 — the honesty invariant was breakable

### 1. A Nalaz caption could assert numbers the view did not produce

`decodeHash`'s story guard seeded its comparison object from the preset's own
patch, so every key the URL omitted compared against **its own value** and passed
vacuously — while the state that actually booted came from `BASE`.

`#v=saldo&c=1&y=2024&st=2` rendered a banner citing **+27.521** over a rail
showing **+41.986** (Sastavnica fell back to `tot`; the caption describes `all`).
Exposed stories 2, 3 and 4 — all carry a non-default `flow`.

**Fixed:** `BASE` moved to the new [src/lib/state.ts](src/lib/state.ts), shared by
App and the codec, so "omitted from the hash" and "still at its default" cannot
drift apart. The guard now compares against `{...BASE, ...decoded}` and runs
*after* the invariant repairs, so a flow hub, `flowSeen` and the jmap year are
part of what gets compared.
*Checks:* `truncated story link is rejected, not rendered with a false caption` ·
`the complete story-2 link still boots its banner over +27.521` ·
`all 7 Nalazi round-trip through their own permalink`

### 2. …and the app emitted such a link itself

`STORY_KEYS` omitted the panel flags, so closing the Državljanstvo panel left
Nalaz 4's caption ("azijski se val hladi") describing a closed panel, and dropped
`cz=1` from the hash while keeping `st=4`.

**Fixed:** `up()` now invalidates on `STORY_KEYS` **plus the active preset's own
patch keys** — Nalaz 4 dies when its panel closes, Nalaz 2 (which says nothing
about panels) survives one being opened.
*Checks:* `closing the panel a Nalaz asserts clears its caption and its st=` ·
`a Nalaz that never mentions a panel survives one being opened`

## P2 — an unclickable control, and a dead-but-focusable one

### 3. The Nalazi banner covered the "Dob i spol" chip at every desktop width

`.storybar` and `.agec` both sat in the map's bottom band; the banner won on
z-index. `elementFromPoint` at the chip's centre returned `storyCap`, and a real
click did nothing — at **1200, 1280, 1366, 1440, 1512 and 1600 px**, for every
preset. Rect overlap was as little as a few hundred px², which is why a
bounding-box sweep missed it.

**Fixed:** the banner moved out of the overlay layer into normal flow under the
map. It now collides with nothing at any width.
*Checks:* `every chip header and close button is actually clickable, 960–1600 px`
(elementFromPoint) · `no map overlay overlaps another at 960–1440 px either`

### 4. Play button was inert but focusable in the JLS view

`opacity:.35 + pointer-events:none` left it `tabbable: true`, `disabled: false` —
a keyboard user tabbed onto a dimmed control and pressed Enter on nothing. The
same defect [Header.tsx](src/components/Header.tsx#L11-L13) documents having
already fixed for the segment groups.

**Fixed:** `disabled={S.view === 'jmap'}`, with `.play:disabled` carrying the dim.
*Check:* `play is disabled (not just dimmed) in the single-year JLS view`

### 5. 25 rail rows announced as buttons did nothing

Regije (5) and JLS (20) rows carried `role="button"` + `tabIndex=0`, but
`activate()` returned immediately for both [`hashChanged: false`].

**Fixed:** `role="button"` only when activating does something. The rows stayed
focusable — and focus now drives the map highlight, which was previously
hover-only, so the highlight became reachable without a pointer.
*Checks:* `inert region rows drop role=button but keep focus + map highlight` ·
`inert JLS rows drop role=button too` · `rows that do open something keep role=button`

## P2 — data presentation

### 6. Matrica: rail row and its own tooltip disagreed on sign

The top neto row read `Osječko-bar. → Grad Zagreb +517`; hovering it produced a
tooltip reading `neto (Osječko-baranjska) −517` and a legend mark at **38.7 %** —
the losing half of the scale.

**Fixed:** a row now separates *what it points at* (`pair`, the gainer's cell —
the one whose displayed value **is** +517) from *how it is worded* (`nm`, always
the direction people moved). Clicking also opens Tokovi hubbed on the gainer, so
the corridor card agrees too.
*Checks:* `matrix neto row and the tooltip it drives report the same sign` ·
`matrix neto row lights the gaining half of the scale`

### 7. Matrica: the rail was byte-identical for Odlasci and Dolasci

Under a header saying "dolasci", the same 20 departure-worded rows.

The list genuinely cannot differ — every directed corridor is one county's
departure and another's arrival, so the network's top 20 is one list seen from
two sides. **Fixed by telling the truth instead of faking a difference:** the
title is now direction-neutral (`koridori · 2018.`) for out/in, only `neto`
names itself, and the rail hint says why. The hub each row points at still
follows Smjer, so the highlighted cell always displays the row's own number.
*Check:* `matrix rail title is direction-neutral for odlasci/dolasci`

## P2 — export fidelity

### 8. Exported PNG/SVG painted solid black bars across the matrix

`.mxband` took `fill:none` from the stylesheet alone and `bakeMapClone` did not
bake it; the export ships no stylesheet, so `fill` fell back to black. Rendered
the exported SVG to a canvas and sampled inside the band: **`rgb(0,0,0)`**.

**Fixed:** the band is baked (`fill`, `stroke`, `stroke-width`, `opacity`), as is
`.arch`'s opacity. The new check is generic rather than specific to this element —
it walks the exported document for *any* painting node with no `fill` and no
`stroke`, so the next CSS-only overlay fails the suite instead of shipping.
*Checks:* `exported document is self-contained (no CSS-only fill/stroke left)` ·
`matrix trace band does not export as a solid black bar` (pixel-sampled)

## P3 — accessibility

### 9. Escape reached two of six dismissible surfaces
Handled `help` and `pair`; the three chip panels and the detail card were the
only surfaces you could open with a key but not close.
**Fixed:** a one-layer-per-press cascade — help → pair → chip panels → detail
card — each handing focus back to the control that owns it. (In Tokovi `sel` is
the hub, not a dismissible selection, so it is left alone.)
*Checks:* `Escape closes the citizenship panel and returns focus to its chip` ·
`Escape closes the detail card and returns focus to its county`

### 10. Every `×` dropped focus to `<body>`
Closing a card unmounted the focused button, restarting Tab from the top.
**Fixed:** `focusSoon()` hands focus to the county, the partner row, the `?`
button or the Nalazi select as appropriate.
*Checks:* `closing the glossary hands focus back to the ? button` · `closing the
detail card hands focus back to its county` · `closing the corridor card hands
focus back to its partner row`

### 11. `role="slider"` with only the arrow keys
Home, End, PageUp and PageDown all did nothing — 27 presses to cross 28 years.
**Fixed:** all four implemented, scoped to `#spark` so Home/End keep their
document meaning everywhere else.
*Check:* `slider implements Home / End / PageDown, not only the arrows`

### 12. Counties said their name; municipalities said their values
`.cnt` → `"Istarska"` against `.jl` → `"Bedenica, Zagrebačka: doseljeno 12,
odseljeno 18, neto −6"`. 21 tab stops on the primary view reading out nothing.
**Fixed:** `metrics.countyAria(S, iso)` builds a per-view label
(`"Istarska: migracijski saldo +22.537 · 2011.–2024."`), and `#tip` — decoration
duplicating those numbers — is now `aria-hidden`.
*Checks:* `county label carries the value, like the JLS map already did` ·
`the cursor tooltip is hidden from AT`

## P3 — layout

### 13. Four collisions in the unmeasured 901–1150 px band
`#legend × #agec` (≤1100), `#card × #agec` (≤1100, up to 19.175 px²),
`#pair × #jcard` (≤940), `#legend × #storyBar` (from 1440 down).

**Fixed structurally, not per-collision:** the two chip panels became one stacked
`.chipdock` — side by side they claimed 656 px of the map's bottom edge, stacked
they claim one panel width at any size and cannot overlap each other either.
`.paircard` drops into flow at ≤960 (where the map box can no longer hold it
beside the 312 px JLS chip) rather than ≤900. The banner move (finding 3) took
the rest. MapView now measures the dock rather than the open panel, so the matrix
grid steers around the collapsed sibling chip too.
*Check:* `no map overlay overlaps another at 960–1440 px either`

### 14. The glossary covered the legend
23.599 px² at 1440×900 — a panel whose first section explains the colour scale,
sitting on the colour scale.
**Fixed:** 164 px of reserve, measured from the tallest legend (klas + %
threshold, 136 px off the map's bottom edge). The glossary scrolls instead.
The same reserve went on `.jcard`, which turned out to have the identical problem
at ≤960 px — found by the new sweep, not by the original review.
*Check:* `open glossary does not cover the legend`

## Verification gaps that let all of this through

The suite was green at 107/107 the whole time. What it could not see:

1. The overlay overlap list omitted `#storyBar`, `#citz`, `#agec`, `#helpCard`.
   → list extended, `.chipdock` added.
2. Geometry ran at 1440 and 390 only; 901–1150 px was unmeasured.
   → overlap + reachability sweeps now run at 960, 1000, 1100, 1200, 1440, 1600.
3. **Rect overlap is not reachability** — the banner covered a control while
   overlapping it by a few hundred px².
   → `elementFromPoint` probes on every chip header and close button.
4. Nothing asserted a decoded `st=` link *renders* the numbers its caption cites.
   → three checks, including a round-trip of all 7 presets.
5. Nothing asserted the exported document is self-contained.
   → generic check for any painting node with no `fill`/`stroke`, plus a pixel sample.

## Checked and already correct

hr-HR formatting and the U+2212 minus throughout; badge wording and the
solid/dashed distinction; per-view `{yi, cum}` memory (round-trips through view
changes *and* Back); threshold abs↔% round-trip; the citizenship out-of-range
clamp note; matrix cumulative badge; autoplay stop-at-end with `aria-pressed`
reset; the county focus indicator (focus applies `.hl`, stroke `#fff 0.8px →
#20262B 1.6px`); `prefers-reduced-motion`; the 390 px pass; zero console errors.

## Known, unchanged

In Tokovi, clicking a new county on the map re-hubs but keeps the corridor card
open, so it silently re-points at a pair the user did not choose (GZ ⇄ Zagrebačka
→ Splitsko-dalm. ⇄ Zagrebačka). Defensible as partner-preservation, and it was
not raised as a defect in the original review — flagged here as a design question
rather than fixed unilaterally.
